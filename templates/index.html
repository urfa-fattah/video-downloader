    How to get cookies for Instagram
    1. In Chrome, Edge or Firefox, install the "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" extension.
    2. Log in to instagram.com in that browser (a secondary account is safer — automated access can get an account restricted).
    3. Stay on instagram.com, click the extension, and Export in Netscape format.
    4. Click Upload cookies.txt above and pick the file. You should see "Instagram login found".

    The file is as sensitive as your password: it stays on this computer in the data folder. Never share it, and delete it when you're done.
